Arduino Uno:
• Microcontroller: ATmega328P
• Operating Voltage: 5V
• Recommended Input Voltage: 7V to 12V
• Input Voltage Limit: 6V to 20V
• Digital I/O Pins: 14 (6 provide PWM output)
• Analog Input Pins: 6 (A0 to A5)
• Clock Speed: 16 MHz
• 32 Flash Memory: 32 KB (0.5 KB used by bootloader)
• SRAM: 2 KB
• EEPROM: 1 KB
• DC Current per I/O Pin: 20 mA (40 mA max on some clone variants like CH340)
• DC Current for 3.3V Pin: 50 mA
• Dimensions: 68.6 mm × 53.4 mm
• Weight: 25 g

• Microcontroller: Tensilica Xtensa Dual-Core 32-bit LX6
• Operating Voltage: 3.3V
• Recommended Input Voltage (VIN): 5V to 12V
• Digital I/O Pins: 25 to 30 usable GPIOs (depending on the exact pinout variant)
• Analog Input Pins (ADC): 15 channels (12-bit resolution)
• Analog Output Pins (DAC): 2 channels (8-bit resolution)
• Clock Speed: Up to 240 MHz
• Flash Memory: 4 MB (external SPI flash)
• SRAM: 520 KB
• Wireless Connectivity: Wi-Fi (802.11 b/g/n) and Bluetooth (v4.2 BR/EDR and BLE)
• Interfaces: SPI, I2C, UART, PWM, I2S, Capacitive Touch

Communication Protocols:
I2C - Inter-Integrated Circuit (low speed)
SPI - Serial Peripheral Interface (high speed)
I2S - Inter-Integrated Circuit Sound
UART - Universal Asynchronous Receiver-Transmitter

I2C	        General chip-to-chip control & sensors	      2	              Half-duplex	Multi-master, Multi-slave	Slower (up to ~3.4 Mbps)
SPI	        High-speed data & displays	              4+	      Full-duplex	Single master, Multi-slave	Fast (10–50+ Mbps)
I2S	        Digital audio streaming	                      3	         Full-duplex / Stream	Single master, Single target	Sample-rate dependent
